Our Story

More Than a Meal — An Experience

iFoods was born from a simple belief: that great food is one of the most profound forms of hospitality. Founded in 2010 by Chef Marco Diaz, our restaurant set out to challenge what dining could mean in a modern world.

Every dish tells a story — of the farmer who grew the ingredient, the chef who transformed it, and the guest who experiences it. We believe in whole-table dining, where conversation flows as freely as the wine, and time slows down just enough to savour what matters.

Over a decade later, we remain committed to our founding philosophy: exceptional seasonal ingredients, honest cooking, and a room that feels like home — just considerably better dressed.

Our Philosophy

The iFoods Principles

🌿

Seasonal First

Our menu changes with the seasons. We work directly with local farms to source what is freshest and most flavourful right now — not what is simply available.

🔥

Timeless Technique

We respect classical cooking methods while embracing modern precision. Every technique we use serves the ingredient — never the other way around.

🤝

Genuine Hospitality

Our service style is warm, knowledgeable, and never stiff. We want every guest to feel genuinely welcome, not merely attended to.
